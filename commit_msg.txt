fix(parseq_head): remove redundant cpu/cuda round-trip that crashes on non-CUDA backends

In the ParseQ refine_iters loop, `tgt_padding_mask` was being explicitly moved
to CPU via `.cpu()` and then back to CUDA via `.cuda()`. This round-trip is
unnecessary — `paddle.cumsum` and type casting work correctly on any device.

On non-CUDA backends (CPU-only, Ascend NPU via Paddle custom device, etc.),
the `.cuda()` call raises an error because Paddle is not compiled with CUDA
support, crashing the inference.

Fix: remove the redundant `.cpu().cuda()` round-trip, keeping the tensor on
its current device throughout.
